## What I expect to find

1. **Co-1 material will be positional and experiential, not metric.** I expect DPO and
   disabled-led publications to say that ramps are too steep, that code-compliant ramps defeat
   independent propulsion, and that assistance is in practice required — and NOT to derive a
   gradient of their own. Prediction: the modal Co-1 document states no number.

2. **Where a Co-1 or T2 document DOES state a gradient, I expect it to be the national code's
   number restated** — 1:12, 1:15, 1:20 — not independently derived. This is the trap of the pass:
   a DPO page quoting 1:12 is evidence about the code's uptake, not evidence for 1:12. If I admit
   such a source I expect to grade the figure `condition` or `finding`, never `claim`, and to say
   in the extraction that its warrant is the code it cites.

3. **Co-2 (OT bodies) is the likeliest place a working value exists.** OTs specify ramps in home
   adaptations, so RCOT/RCOTSSH (Housing Adaptations Without Delay), AOTA, CAOT and OT Australia
   are where a practice-level gradient preference would live. I expect practice guidance that
   cites the statutory maximum and then prefers something shallower. If a Co-2 document states a
   preferred gradient with a stated basis, that is the highest-value admission this pass can make.

4. **The strongest available finding is an INADEQUACY finding, not a value.** I expect the genuine
   content here to be that the code-permitted maximum is unusable for a substantial share of
   wheelchair users. Under R7 and the 2026-09-13 ruling ("a finding that a CODE is insufficient
   supplies no value, and is still first-class evidence") that is first-class, and it bears
   directly on the threshold half the owner's 2026-09-16 statement says no admitted source
   supplies. Prediction: I find qualitative threshold claims ("too steep to self-propel") and NOT
   a quantitative threshold of acceptability.

5. **Non-English jurisdictions will be harder to retrieve than to search.** I expect DPO sites to
   publish PDFs behind navigation rather than indexed pages, and I expect more failures of
   RETRIEVAL than of search. Per R14 I will distinguish, per execution: genuine absence
   (the organisation publishes nothing on this) / wrong index (searched the wrong surface) /
   query-shape failure / retrieval failure (found but could not fetch). These are four different
   results and only the first is evidence of absence.

6. **I expect some real zeroes and I will keep them.** A jurisdiction whose DPOs publish nothing
   on ramp gradient is a completed unit of work, not a failed one.

## What would surprise me

- A DPO or OT body publishing a **primary participatory study** of gradient preference or
  tolerability. If one exists it is co-primary with T1 under CRPD Art 4.3 and is the most valuable
  source available on this cell.
- Any Co-1/T2/Co-2 document supplying a **quantitative threshold of acceptability** — a stated
  gradient above which use fails, with a warrant that is not simply the code. That is the missing
  half of the determination, and I do not expect to find it.
- Convergence between jurisdictions on a number OTHER than their own codes'.
