### 1.2 Evidence grammar

Every claim carries one of: `file:line` (resolved with `grep -n -F` on the day of writing); a query or command and what it returned; a verbatim quote with its source line. Every claim is marked `VERIFIED` (the writer ran or read it that day), `INFERRED` (basis named) or `UNVERIFIED`. A claim copied from an audit file without re-running its command is `INFERRED from <file> §x`.

Database access, always: `sqlite3.connect('file:data/guidebook.db?mode=ro', uri=True)` under `python3 -I`. Record `sha256sum data/guidebook.db` at the start and end of each round in the document's resolution record; it must not change.

### 1.3 The agonist–antagonist method

- **Agonist.** Writes the document as numbered positions (`P-<doc>-<n>`): one claim or proposal each, with its evidence and status mark. The agonist files the complete affirmative case; nothing is held back for a reply.
- **Antagonist.** A separate context that has not read the agonist's working, only the draft and the sources. It attacks each position through named lenses: **existence** (does the cited thing exist at the cited place today), **fidelity** (does the quote or row say what the position says), **supersession** (is a later ruling in the way), **subject** (did the cited check examine anything; a zero-row object is unproven), **count** (is any number typed), **warrant class** (owner quote, ledger reading, contract, relayed, memory note, assumption), **assumption import**, **owner gate** (is something decided that is the owner's), **redness** (could the proposed test fail on today's data; run it), **prior work** (does an existing plan already say this). The antagonist must re-run at least the commands this brief lists for the document, before reading the agonist's figures, and then compare (blind, then compare).
- **Resolution record.** The last section of every document, one row per position: `position | antagonist objection (lens) | evidence for the objection | disposition`. Dispositions: `upheld` (objection failed; say why), `amended` (text changed; say how), `withdrawn`, `referred to owner` (becomes or joins an OD entry). Positions the antagonist attacked and could not fault are recorded as `upheld — attacked on <lenses>`; a record that shows only sustained objections is incomplete.
- **One round.** No reply to the antagonist, no pass on the pass. Disputes go to the owner register.
- **Finish.** A document is finished when its acceptance criteria (per document, below) hold and its resolution record has no position without a disposition.
