Plan v1 does not satisfy the owner's requirements as drawn: it harvests names, not figures, before it renders; it leaves every slug coupling that matters (search, coverage, mining, candidates, determination record) untouched; and it mass-promotes into a vocabulary with no retire, merge, rename or un-decline path. Per requirement — (1) pipeline quality/logic/sequencing/gates: **FAIL** (Release 0 depends on extractions no phase before render writes; P1/P2 cannot ship as separate PRs under the blocking DoD). (2) Slug-independent scraping: **PARTIAL** (extraction keys on parameter and tolerates cross-slug reads — verified in code — but coverage, candidates, mining and the 2026-09-28 exhaustion ruling are per slug and no slug→parameter mapping exists). (3) Multi-term harvest per source: **FAIL** (one observation per phrase per source; no scan ledger; the quote check the plan cites for observations does not exist). (4) Expandable catalogue: **FAIL** (every expansion path that changes an existing row is a compensating migration).
