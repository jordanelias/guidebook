## Refusals — decided now, so they are not decided under pressure later

1. **No value is filed that is not in persisted bytes.** Batch 10 saw the French figures through a reader tool and did not file them. Same rule here, every row.
2. **A landing page is not a held identity.** One BSI or ISO catalogue address serves a family and resolves to many ref_ids.
3. **No determination is recomputed.** Determination 3 is retired. Even if ISO or the IPC guide clears richness outright, recomputing publishes a regulatory floor as though it answered the owner's question. ACTION (2) of 2026-09-16 is engine work and is not this batch.
4. **Co-1 is not waived with `CO1-NOT-APPLICABLE`.** Four Co-1 routes are planned; the threshold of acceptability is precisely what lived experience answers.
5. **A zero-yield search is a completed unit of work** and is kept, with R14's four-way diagnosis stated: genuine absence / wrong index / query-shape failure / retrieval failure. A paywall is the fourth, never the first.
6. **`add-source --local-ref-id` on every admission.** Batch 10 lost an admission to the phantom-link defect; the writer now refuses, but the habit is the real fix. Next unused for this slug is 13.
