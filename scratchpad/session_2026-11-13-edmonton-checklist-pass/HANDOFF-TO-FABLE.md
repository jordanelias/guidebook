### A3 (to Q3): how the base grows dynamically
Today's mechanism: (1) harvest every concept phrase at every reading (`observe-term`, verbatim, sentence, locator; R11); (2) adjudicate each (names existing, not ours, deferred; a term is minted only from an observation, in the source's own words, never a phrase the model generates: OR-17, E1); (3) promote or decline (`add-parameter`, `decline-parameter`, `rename-term`, `merge-parameter`); (4) re-read debt is automatic (a new parameter widens the catalogue version and `v_scan_ledger` owes a targeted re-read of every held source); (5) guards (near-duplicate refusal, flat and coarse parameters OD-24, a kind needs a warrant T-020). Where it stalls: DEFERRED has no consumer; T-072 only seeds from regulatory codes; no recurrence signal; minting waits for a hand-built review; pending cells do not feed back. Proposal in order of value: a derived candidate queue (every unminted observation grouped by phrase and concept with figures, number of independent sources, their tiers, locators; recurrence across independent sources with a stated figure ranks first), a generated review sheet for the owner's bulk reading, a standing catalogue-growth batch (T-072 widened), and a feedback hook so a cell pending for a missing parameter writes a candidate back. Decisions put to the owner: is recurrence enough to propose minting (owner still approves); should an element-only phrase with no figure ever be mined; what cadence or threshold triggers a batch. Coordinator's leaning: recurrence as a ranking signal only, element-only phrases not mined, a growth batch whenever the queue holds a stated figure no parameter takes.

### A4 (to Q4): not duplicating
What exists: `add-term` compares the new name against every term and every attested English alias (`_near_terms` in `scripts/db.py`; token-set subset or superset, a one-token term inside a compound does not count) and refuses unless each match is named `--distinct-from` with a rationale; an exact name hit goes to the existing term; aliases are typed (SYNONYM, BROADER, NARROWER, TRANSLATION, DOMAIN, DEPRECATED) with an attestation (instrument, model_seed, other_source, owner_supplied, unrecorded), a model_seed alias blocks nothing; `merge-parameter` and `rename-term` undo a duplicate afterwards. What it misses: it compares words only (a synonym with no shared token is not caught), names only (not definitions, quantity kinds or units), no cross-language synonyms (E1: an instrument's own phrase counts as the name). Equivalence is a recorded judgment with a rationale; in the digest, adjudicating "doorway clearance" as existing term TERM-021 (door width) was one such judgment and is contestable. Proposed: a derived candidate list beside each proposed term, ranked by overlap of name, definition, quantity kind, unit dimension and direction (depends on T-020, T-028). The coordinator did not check whether `rename-term`, `merge-parameter` or the alias types behave exactly as stated beyond reading the help and `_near_terms`.

### A5 (to Q5): branch of a parameter versus its own
Coordinator's table: same quantity (same kind and unit dimension, same better-direction), different circumstance: same parameter, circumstance goes in `--setting`, lens, device class, or an element pointer (T-060); applies only under a condition: a branch of the determination via a `condition_on` edge (T-017 keeps it out of the headline); different quantity (width vs height vs length vs time vs count vs force): its own parameter even on the same element; a thing, not a measure: declined as an element (`decline-parameter`, the TERM-089 `ramp` precedent); a threshold that triggers a rule: a condition on that rule's figure, not a value of anything; a passage in the source: neither, clauses are what is read (clause inventory behind T-031, OD-23). Examples from the PDF: path width 1500 (item 1.2) and corridor 1100 (item 2.10) one quantity two settings (parameter 4); elevator door 910 to 915 is parameter 9 in an elevator setting; the 9 m landing interval is parameter 12; "handrails on both sides if the stair is 1100 mm or wider" is a condition on a handrail rule, not a stair-width parameter; the access aisle's 1500 and 6000 are two quantities, width and length. **Known error in the digest, admitted in this answer:** the coordinator promoted TERM-092 `intermediate landing` and TERM-022 `level threshold` to parameters (13 and 14); both names are elements, not quantities, contradicting the TERM-089 decline reasoning; the critic found the same.

### A6 (to Q6): values per group and design modes
A determination is one parameter combined with its lens codes (identity, access need, ICF, medical; at least one; unique). A figure reaches a cell only through its own live lens links (T-019), so a value for MOB and one for BLIND sit in different cells. A parameter whose direction is `contested` shows the spread with each population's direction and never one invented value; a conflict goes in `add-conflict`. `specifications.design_scale` takes `universal`, `population`, `person`: the engine writes `universal` when only code-stratum (T4 to T6) evidence stands behind the cell (assess_cell.py G1) and `population` otherwise; `person` is OT-assessed and the guidebook gives no values and no person-assessment form (governance/adversarial-use-framework.md). Coordinator found no rule that derives a universal value from the per-group cells.

### A7 (to Q7): commercial, residential, public
The owner ruled on 2026-08-27 that `base_building` has three levels: building type (residential, commercial), room type, construction element; `rooms` (17 rows) exists, the other two levels are absent; the building-type level is also deferred in the conceptual model (ENT-16). Handling today: it lives as free text in each figure's `setting`; nothing keys or filters a cell by it, so a dwellings figure and a non-dwellings figure for the same parameter and lens can land in one determination. OD-24 says carry such variations as typed conditions; T-017 turns a conditioned claim into a branch; the typed vocabulary and registry are what is missing. "Public" is the instrument's own legal scope word and differs by country. **Not verified by the coordinator:** whether `room_items` or any other junction for the three levels still exists after the item-layer drop (the ledger text said 0 rows); whether any live figure rows carry a typed building-type field (they do not appear to).

### A8 (to Q8): too granular versus too encompassing
The split test (OD-24): two cases are two parameters only when they cannot be placed on one scale or their accessibility directions differ; everything else is one parameter, with clause variations as typed conditions and group differences as lenses; the reason for coarse is the owner's 2026-11-12 statement that too fine a grain loses the commonality needed to "unify then specify". Signs of too encompassing: the cell's figures split systematically by setting, or a group's difference is lost (the OD-24 reversal condition; it shows as branches, divergence rows, contested spreads). Signs of too granular: near-duplicate parameters sharing a kind and direction, a parameter no two instruments can both fill. An element never earns a parameter. The medical vocabulary: the owner's 2026-09-11 doubt that it was "too granular to be usable" turned that lens into a demand-populated resolver, not a mirror taxonomy. Open per the ledger: how the grain is represented (families, equivalence groups), who judges a grouping, and whether a granularity policy is doctrine or convention (marked OWNER DECISION REQUIRED in the audit's improvement programme).

### A9 (to Q9): combining parameters that belong together
Parameters stay separate. You answered OR-14b: the specifications pass writes an element-level determination from the related parameters' determinations: a new stage output (a table and a rule version) built in a tooling pull request; the element is a view over them, never a container figures are filed into; parameters reach an element through typed term pointers, many to many (OD-24). `condition_on` and `derived_from` carry a dependency between figures across parameters; `connections` rows (cross-parameter, compound-interaction) record the narrative; T-060 is the task that would make the element page read the edges and connections. Example: item 4.7 (a stair needs a handrail on one side, handrails on both sides if 1100 mm or wider): the 1100 mm is a condition on the handrail requirement, a `condition_on` edge; items 7.1 and 7.3 (handrail diameter 30 to 43 mm, height 865 to 965 mm) are separate quantities under the element "handrail". Today nothing reads `connections` or `conflicts` (the skill says the only reader is the operator at the adversarial pass; whether the engine should consume them is open owner question 12 of the 2026-11-06 round-3 adjudication); T-060's design file is not written; stair width, handrail height and diameter are among the 57 deferred phrases, so the example cannot run until they are minted.
