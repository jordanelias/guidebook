## The owner's questions, verbatim

**Q1.** "how does this pipeline handle identifying new topics for research, conducting them in another language, verifying a source is reputable, adjudicating how much of the source is relevant across different topics, writing down everything relevant including new topics if they don't already exist, judging the parameters for their values as well as assigning their codes as well as assigning evidence tier as well as determining category, and then synthesizing everything within the topic so that it can form a specification/determination?"

**Q2.** "is there an iterative loop with limited repetitions to ensure exit such that you can ensure that topics across languages and synonyms are properly consolidated and associated in base with those consolidations propagating forward throughout database so we don't end up with duplicates or near identical topics?"

**Q3.** "you have identified missing items. what else exists in pipeline that allows us to work in all directions and systematically perform new passes over already-recorsed rows to ensure that the database codes/terms/etc are consistently applied as well as to append existing rows with more information as required if the base gets extended?"

**Q4.** "in fact, how does the pipeline handle propagation in all directions whenever there is a change made to a row or a new row somewhere is made?"

## What the pipeline does today (checked)

**Q1 (flow).** Seven stages (`governance/pipeline-contract.yaml`, `stages:`): base, research, evidence, judgment, synthesis, specification, render, with ruled backward edges (T-022 writes them into the contract, on the stacked branch). Topics are `slugs`, authored from the ICF/access-need frame, never from the retired item list (DR-2026-08-19 section 1.4). A new concept enters as `observe-term` (verbatim, unjudged), is settled by `adjudicate-term` (outcomes NAMES-NEW, NAMES-EXISTING, DEFERRED, NOT-OURS), then `add-term` / `add-parameter` (needs a quantity kind and a warranting observation since T-020, stacked branch) or `decline-parameter`. Language: a search carries language and jurisdiction; coverage is per parameter and bucket member (`lang_jur_map`); no back-translation (R11); translations have a typed table (T-049, stacked branch), shown only when reviewed (the reader gate is T-037, not built). Reputable: no flag; verification (R10 re-retrieval, logged retrieval manifest, author fidelity check) plus tier computed from evidence type and scope (`derive_tier`, checked by `adjudication_integrity`); type and scope are judged. Relevance across topics: R16, every admission read against the whole parameter list (`record-scan`, `source_scans`); relevance is extraction rows per parameter, not a source score. Values: `source_value_extractions`. Codes: identity, ICF and access-need columns on the extraction and `extraction_lens_links`, written by judgment (the maps only propose); three lenses since the medical lens was deleted on 2026-11-16. "Category": not verified as one field. Synthesis: `convergence_assessment`, Opus-only; determination: `specifications` keyed on parameter plus at least one lens column, computed by the engine (`pilot-7`), not hand-assigned.

**Q2 (consolidation).** There is NO iterative, bounded consolidation loop. What exists: a write-time near-duplicate refusal (`db.py _near_terms`: token-set subset or superset against every canonical name and every alias not `model_seed`, in any language; refused unless `--distinct-from TERM-ID` with a rationale); adjudication NAMES-EXISTING; `merge-parameter` (re-points every foreign key into the folded parameter, table list read from the live schema; refuses a live determination or open gate), `rename-term`, `retire-parameter`. Bounded iteration exists only for citation mining depth and a recorded `saturation_signal` on searches.

**Q3 (passes over recorded rows).** Owed work is DERIVED, not executed: `v_scan_ledger` (one row per unsuperseded source and active parameter; status EXTRACTED, STATED-ABSENT, READ-NOTHING-FOUND, BLOCKED, or not read / partly read), `v_reading_owed`, the advisory `scan_debt` (no baseline by design, red whenever debt > 0), `v_deferred_acts` (a deferral stands while its cell is owed), `v_coverage_priority` and the other `v_coverage_*` views, `unmined`, `unmined-gaps`, `cell-status`, `coverage`. Adding to a recorded figure is by new pointer rows (`add-lens-link`, `retire-lens-link`, `add-population-icf-link`, `retire-identity` with a successor), retire-in-place (specifications; admission edges and connections once T-050 merges), and narrow `amend-*` verbs (`amend-extraction` is reformat-only). Read-only audits: `column_vocabulary_audit`, `schema_reference_audit`, `identifier_floor_audit`, `alias_provenance_audit`, `adjudication_integrity`, `extraction_relations_integrity`, `derivation_handshake_integrity`, `derived_not_curated_audit`, `graph_audit`, `test_db_integrity`, `migration_reproducibility`.

**Q4 (propagation).** By pull and by refusal, not push. The schema has ONE trigger (`trg_spec_retire_needs_reason`). A change reaches others because they point at the row (rule 5; 29 views), through cascades in one place (`merge-parameter`, successors, `merged_into` followed by `v_deferred_acts`), through writers that refuse until dependents are handled (merge/retire refuse live determinations and open gates), through determinations as recomputable caches (`derivation_sha` hashes the graded link set and each figure's in-lens state and the three lens codes; `derivation_handshake_integrity` recomputes), through the capture path (scratch copy, `dbcore.writable_tables`, `emit_batch_sql`, migration only) and replay checks, and through read-only audits.

## The gaps identified (G-numbers are this file's, for the adjudication to cite)

- **G1.** No bounded, exit-guaranteed consolidation sweep for topics/terms/synonyms across languages.
- **G2.** No `merge-term` verb (only `merge-parameter`, `rename-term`, `retire-parameter`) [UNVERIFIED: the verb list was read by name, not every parser].
- **G3.** No cross-language concept identity: a concept is keyed on an English string. Tasks that address it and were open when the plan was generated: T-028 (concept identity, NOT FOR SONNET), T-029 (typed pointers between terms), T-057 (base coverage view), T-069 (promote adjudicated non-English observations to forms); T-028-design.md exists.
- **G4.** The near-match gate skips `model_seed` aliases; live aliases are all `model_seed` or `unrecorded` (derive: `select attestation, count(*) from term_aliases group by 1`), none attested, so a cross-language near-duplicate is caught only through an unrecorded alias.
- **G5.** Owed passes are listed, not executed: no scheduler, no bounded re-pass, no exit rule; `scan_debt` and `verified_scan_integrity` are red today.
- **G6.** Re-assigning lens codes to old figures when the base grows is a judgment; the writer's proposals (T-126) are unbuilt; OD-67 (the population-to-ICF rows) was referred back to the owner.
- **G7.** Propagation is detected by audit, not on write; several audits are advisory; migrations, the verification cron and unknown routes bypass writer refusals.
- **G8.** `derivation_sha` does not hash a source's verification and tier, a row's grading and comparator, or a `confirms` edge (stated in `scripts/audit/derivation_handshake_integrity.py`); a stored determination can be stale without the sha moving.
- **G9.** Tolerated second homes: `specifications.functional_basis` (a JSON snapshot) and a gate's `trigger_tier`.
- **G10.** Prose callers and generated files are not swept by a gate (CLAUDE.md rule 4; `context_map_fresh` covers the context map only).
- **G11.** `python3 workplan/pipeline-state-graph/build_graph.py --check` reports about a hundred failures on the base, unchanged by each task of the stacked branch; `gaps` is `cross-stage` in the stage map but `walkability.py` leaves a cross-stage table outside the spine (OD-11 "count it as a member of each stage it carries" is not met).
- **G12.** "Category" in Q1 is ambiguous: evidence type, claim type, a term's `domain`, quantity kind or `design_scale`; the owner was asked which and has not said.
- **G13.** Task-level findings of the stacked branch that are the owner's or a design question are in `scratchpad/session_2026-11-16-remediation-batch-1-tooling-stack/sweep-list.md` (one section per task: T-020, T-022, T-049, T-050, T-089 once written, and T-093). Several bear on propagation: the translation view cannot hide a stale translation (no hash in SQL; T-037 must check), `retirement_reason` has no reader, readers of `connections` and `search_admissions` needed filters added by hand, the graph extractor ignores `re_entry`.
- **G14.** DR-2026-08-19 section 12.5 lists "tier and evidence-type assignment" as permanently manual; CLAUDE.md rule 8 derives the tier from type and scope. The reconciliation (type and scope judged, tier computed) was stated, not recorded in a ruling.
