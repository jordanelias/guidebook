## What it does
- **Migration 116** adds `specifications.marker` with two column CHECKs: the vocabulary and "a regulatory-stratum-only row is never above the weak band"; and "a live stated or provisional row carries a marker, a live pending or not_applicable row carries none" (retired rows exempt). `ADD COLUMN`, not a rebuild: the sequence, the lens CHECK, the inbound keys, the index, the trigger and the other views stay as they are. `v_best_practice` is redefined without `strength_band` (no code read it; `SELECT *` carries `marker`). Declared `-- AFTER_DATA`. **Rehearsed first** on a scratch copy: every existing row is retired, so both CHECKs hold; a live provisional row with no marker, a pending row with a marker, a regulatory row above the weak band and a glyph outside the vocabulary are each refused.
- **Engine.** `assess_cell.determination_marker` computes it after the state is settled (so after the H4 cap): each governing source takes its section 5 band (`schemas.tier_derivation.band`) and the cell the floor of the mean (`floor_mean`). A value read off the regulatory stratum, a floor claim or the threshold of a proxy inference, is the weak band unless an anchoring *finding* confirms the governing code through a `confirms` edge to a governing extraction *row* (R39); that code then takes the confirming study's band and the cell the floor-mean of its codes. A code counts once however many of its clauses govern. `main` writes the column; `EvidenceStateRecord` types it; the report line prints it.
- **A bug found by running it and fixed in the same change.** `main`'s final print loop read `r["excluded_not_publishable"]`, which the report dict never carried, so every run raised `KeyError` after the rows were committed to the scratch database and the SQL emitted. It fails identically on the previous commit. The key is now in the report.
- **End to end on scratch copies** (`assess_cell.py --parameter-id 3 --identity MOB`): the new row is `provisional ○` (a proxy inference whose threshold no finding confirms); two copies emit byte-identical SQL; `validate_evidence_state.py --db` passes. `data/guidebook.db` moved only through `migrate_db.py`.
- **Tests.** `test_assess_cell_pilot`: the marker CHECKs asserted against the positive control; engine cases for Co-2 stated (full), T6-only (weak), pending (none), T3-clinical (provisional, full) and T3-grey (pending, none); the confirmation route with all three codes confirmed (full), two of three (partial), one (weak), a `tested_at` edge and a `confirms` edge aimed at a label (both weak). `test_evidence_cell_state_2_3` and `test_validate_evidence_state_2_4` give every live stated or provisional fixture row a marker from `EvidenceMarker`, so their refusal assertions still fire on the rule they name and not on the new CHECK; `test_db_amend_writers` X12 and E10 un-retire with the weak band (legal on any row) and E10 restores it.
- **Prose.** The three callers of `strength_band` (`evidence-architecture.md` twice, `evidence-methodology.md` once) now name `marker`, the self-referential correction notes beside them are deleted, atoms EVI-182 and EVI-333 follow; the mission's marker line gains "a determination carries the band `assess_cell` computes" (atom MIS-03), which is now true. An engine comment that narrated migration 027 and 029 is cut to its one live sentence. **Owner merges:** the three governance files above.

## Decided here, not built
- **`determination_gates` stays identity-only; `P-GT` stays FAIL.** Its readers are `assess_cell.derivation_handshake` (by parameter and identity), `derivation_handshake_integrity`, and the raise and resolve verbs. A gate is raised against a parameter "by an audit that may not know which cells exist yet", and `identity_code NULL` means every lens, so it cannot point at a determination (rule 5's alternative) and adding the other three lens columns would need an engine reader that does not exist and a change to what NULL means. LED-128 says any table attaching a determination to a group takes four lens columns and one CHECK; whether that reaches a gate, whose claim is the identity-to-ICF derivation, is the owner's. **Owner item.**
- **Not built, as the plan records:** the band of a `figure_role='derived'` row from its inputs (0 rows); a T3-clinical confirming study (the CHECK pins such a cell at the weak band); `derivation_sha` does not cover `extraction_relations`, so a `confirms` edge added after a determination changes what the engine would write without moving the sha. ◐ cannot appear on a determination the engine writes today: stated sets are full, regulatory sets are weak, and the middle arises only from partial confirmation or a derived figure.
