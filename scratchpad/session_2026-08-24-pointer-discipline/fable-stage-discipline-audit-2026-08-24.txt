### D1 — CRITICAL (live rows, live drift, live reader bug)
**`citation_mining.doi` and `citation_mining.local_ref_id`** | duplicated values: the source's DOI and its per-slug bibliography label | authoritative copies: `evidence_sources.doi` (via `citation_mining.global_ref_id`, populated in **all 10 rows**) and `source_slug_links.local_ref_id` | rows affected: 10 | **AGREE? NO, twice over**: (i) `citation_mining.doi` = `10.1044/2019_AJA-19-0010` / `2016_AJA-15-0064` vs `evidence_sources.doi` lowercase for REF-00325/REF-00578; (ii) `local_ref_id` disagrees on 3/10 rows — ssl `RAP-06`/`RAP-09`/`RAP-10` vs cm `RAP-F61`/`RAP-F69`/`RAP-F70` (REF-00561/00969/00970).

**The drift is not cosmetic — it breaks a reader today.** `scripts/db.py` `get_unmined_sources()` (line ~504) and the tier-priority query (line ~1711) both do:
```sql
LEFT JOIN citation_mining cm ON cm.slug=ssl.slug AND cm.local_ref_id=ssl.local_ref_id
```
For the 3 divergent rows the join misses, `cm.backward` comes back NULL, and three *already-mined* sources are reported as unmined — an agent following that list re-mines them. The writer is `log_mining()` (line ~245), which takes `local_ref_id` and `doi` as caller-supplied strings.

- **AGONIST:** drift has already happened, in both columns, within one 10-row pipeline, and it already misleads a shipping reader. `global_ref_id` is populated everywhere; the pointer exists and is simply not being dereferenced.
- **ANTAGONIST:** (a) frozen snapshot? No — a mining ledger's identity column is not a point-in-time citation; the thing mined is the evidence row itself. (d) pointer nothing dereferences? No — the readers exist and are two `db.py` functions; the fix names them.
- **VERDICT: AGONIST WINS.** Join on `cm.global_ref_id = ssl.ref_id`; stop writing `cm.doi`; `cm.local_ref_id` survives only as the mining session's own label (or is repaired to match ssl), never as a join key.

2. **Do not drop `reference_stubs`** — the empty tombstone is load-bearing for replay: `data_20260823223839` writes into it. 062 already tried the tidy version and failed the reproducibility gate.

Append-only + replay makes them permanent as *shapes*; retire writers and readers, NULL forward. 5. **Do not add parity checks, triggers, or new CHECK apparatus to police the remaining copies** — CLAUDE.md §1: apparatus carries the burden of proof, and D2 shows where parity checks lead: they make dual-homes *survivable*, which makes them *permanent*. The discipline is fewer homes, not better-guarded homes.
6. **Do not execute the `axes` retirement inside this dedup series** — it is real duplication (D9) but it is an owner-ruling execution with 232+ live crossing rows and its own blast radius; mixing it into pointer hygiene would hide a doctrinal change inside plumbing.
7. **Do not build junctions for `convergence_assessment`'s JSON arrays yet** — zero rows, zero readers, and a defensible frozen-assessment semantics; a pointer nothing dereferences is worse than a copy (antagonist d).
