**Key:** *kind* is the kind of what the row would mint, or of the subject where nothing is minted (quantity, element, intervention, method, setting, lens). *Prescription-bearing* names the clause the value regex cannot see. *Held as* is the screen's own column (exact name or alias match); a nearer holding found by hand is in the reason. *Proposed name* is a contiguous part of the stripped subject, checked by script (case-insensitive) and checked against every term name and every alias in every language: no MINT row fails either test. *Implied quantity* is prose, never a name to mint. MINT+PROMOTE = `mint-term-from-catalogue` then `add-parameter`; MINT+DECLINE = mint then `decline-parameter`; HOLD-LEAD = record the implied quantity for an open pass, mint nothing; REJECT = already held, a prescription with no clean part, a method or lens, or not ours.
