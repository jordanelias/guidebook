**Owner classes in Spanish (Spain).**
- *Parking.* Building code: "Plaza de aparcamiento accesible" (DB-SUA anejo A) and "plaza accesible". Regulation: "Plazas accesibles de aparcamiento" and "plazas de aparcamiento para personas con movilidad reducida" (RD 505). Public space: "Plazas de aparcamiento reservadas para personas con movilidad reducida" (TMA art. 35; also road-sign catalogue 2.8, RD 465/2025). Permit-keyed: "… para personas titulares de la tarjeta de estacionamiento" (RD 1056/2014 art. 5). Municipal: "reservas especiales de estacionamiento" (Bornos) and "plazas de estacionamiento reservado para personas con movilidad reducida (PMR)" (Madrid). Disfavoured: "minusválidos" on Seville plates, which CERMI asks to have replaced. Forms seen only in forums and driving-school blogs ("zonas de minusválidos", "señal de minusválido") were not admitted.
- *Umbrella qualifier.* The code term is "accesible". The statutory terms are "accesibilidad universal" and "diseño universal o diseño para todas las personas" (RDL 1/2013 art. 2 k–l), both WIDER. "Diseño para todos" is the 2007 form, and "eliminación / supresión de barreras (arquitectónicas)" names the earlier regional-law generation. The user-group qualifiers "personas con movilidad reducida" and "PMR" are NARROWER. "Adaptado" is NARROWER. "Accesibilidad cognitiva" is NARROWER and in force only from 2027-01-02. "Minusválidos" was replaced by statute (Ley 39/2006 DA 8ª). No source read here attests "sin barreras", "inclusivo" as a qualifier of a built object, "wheelchair friendly" or a colloquial "aseo adaptado".
- *Vertical transport.* The lift is "Ascensor accesible", defined differently in DB-SUA (by UNE-EN 81-70) and in RD 505 (by features). The platforms are "plataforma elevadora vertical" and "plataforma elevadora inclinada (salvaescaleras)" (DA DB-SUA/2, a guidance tier), and RD 505 has "plataformas salvaescaleras". "Sillas salvaescaleras" is the seat stair lift, which DA DB-SUA/2 does not accept as an effective adaptation. The class words are "elementos y dispositivos mecánicos" and the regulation's "aparatos elevadores". The other devices are "grúa para piscina", "escaleras mecánicas" and "andenes móviles". As a noun for a lift, "elevador" occurs 0 times in the 14 readable texts held here; the one hit is the adjective in "Reglamento de aparatos elevadores".

**Conflations (14 notes; the most telling):** (1) "ascensores" is used of certain vertical platforms (DA DB-SUA/2 fn. 2, pdf p.6), which is the lift = elevator or platform conflation, stated by a Spanish source. (2) Bare "salvaescaleras" names the inclined platform (DA p.6), while UNE-EN 81-40's cited title lists "Salvaescaleras y plataformas elevadoras inclinadas" as two devices, and "sillas salvaescaleras" is a third. (3) "Plaza" names a car bay and a wheelchair spectator place in one DB-SUA ratio sentence (SUA 9, 1.2.3). (4) TMA art. 35 heads its article "movilidad reducida" but keys the body to permit holders "con discapacidad", and Madrid's PMR class takes in visual disability. (5) "Practicable" means openable in DB-SUA and usable in RDL 1/2013. (6) "Vado" means a kerb ramp and a vehicle crossing. (7) DB-SUA fuses two symbol names into "Símbolo Internacional de Accesibilidad para la movilidad (SIA)".
