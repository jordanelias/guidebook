## The owner classes in Norwegian
- **Parking.** The code term is "parkeringsplasser for forflytningshemmede" (TEK17 § 8-8 (1)). It names the user group, not the umbrella word. The road authority writes "parkeringsplasser reservert for forflytningshemmede". Nynorsk has "plass for rørslehemma" (Årdal). Colloquial and DPO usage is HC-: "HC-parkering", "HC-parkeringsplass", "HC plasser" (NHF). The permit is a separate object: "parkeringstillatelse for forflytningshemmede", which the road authority itself says is "iblant omtalt som HC‑kort". Nynorsk has "parkeringsløyve for forflyttingshemma" (road authority) and "parkeringsløyve for rørslehemma" (Årdal).
- **Umbrella qualifier.** The instrument's word is "universell utforming" / "universelt utformet", a defined higher level. "tilgjengelig" is a defined lower level for dwellings. These are graded levels, not synonyms. Other forms: "tilrettelagt" (on parking), Kartverket's graded "Tilgjengelig / Delvis tilgjengelig / Ikke tilgjengelig", "UU", the older framing "Tilgjengelighet for alle" (T-5/99 B), and the HC- prefix. "rullestolvennlig" and "handikapvennlig" remain unattested seeds (W5).
- **Vertical transport.** "heis" means the elevator and "heisstol" its car. "løfteplattform" is the platform lift (§ 15-12), which may replace an elevator (§ 12-3 (1)). "trappeheis" is the stair lift. The umbrella "løfteinnretning" covers all of them, escalators included. Unlike CA/US "lift", Norwegian puts "heis" on the narrower devices: nav.no's "sjaktheis" is a platform lift in a shaft, and "Heis med sete eller plattform" is a stair lift.

## Conflations (14 rows carry a CONFLATION note: `awk -F'\t' 'NR>1 && $12 ~ /CONFLATION/' $D/equivalents.tsv | wc -l`)
The most consequential: "heis" covers the elevator (TEK17), a shafted platform lift (nav.no, which contrasts it with "vanleg heis" in the same sentence) and a stair lift. "tilgjengelig" gets two different design bases on one DiBK page. It also carries a non-disability sense ("lett tilgjengelig for drift", § 15-10) and a generic sense in the permit regulation's § 1 ("tilgjengelig parkeringstilbud", quoted by Bærum). "HC-parkering" names both the space and the permit scheme. NHF contrasts "HC løsninger" with "universell utforming". "rullestolheis" and "rampe" name vehicle devices ("rampe i bilen"). The handrail is "håndløper" in TEK17 and "håndlist" in TEK10 and Kartverket.
