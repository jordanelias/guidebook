## Where Italian carves the field differently (instrument-derived; glosses ours)
1. **Three graded outcomes, not one.** DM art. 2-3 define "accessibilità", "visitabilità" and "adattabilità" ("un'accessibilità differita") as "tre livelli di qualità dello spazio costruito". Which grade applies depends on building type (art. 3.2-3.5). The register has no axis for this. "Visitabilità" (access to the "spazi di relazione" and one toilet) has no English register row.
2. **A three-layer drafting with an equivalence route.** The structure is criteri di progettazione (art. 4-6, aims), then specificazioni with "valore prescritto" (art. 8), then "soluzioni tecniche conformi" (art. 9, deemed to comply). Alternatively, "soluzioni alternative" may be offered, with "equivalente o migliore qualità degli esiti" (art. 7). Each aim clause points to its specification ("Per le specifiche vedi 8.1.x").
3. **"Adeguamento" relaxes the minima.** Combined manoeuvres, smaller lift cars, steeper ramps by graph, and removing the bidet are all permitted "nei casi di adeguamento". One parameter therefore has a new-build value and an adaptation value.
4. **Force is a pressure in kg.** "esercitando una pressione non superiore a 8 kg" applies to doors and windows; "forza" does not occur in this sense. The held aliases "forza di azionamento" and "forza operativa" occur 0 times.
5. **Turning is a manoeuvre, not a circle.** The text uses "spazi di manovra" (diagrams), "inversione di marcia", "manovra di rotazione", and once, for balconies, an inscribable "circonferenza di diametro 140 cm". The held "raggio di rotazione" and "spazio di manovra" occur 0 times.
6. **Headroom has no noun.** It is "Fino ad un'altezza minima di 2.10 m dal calpestio, non devono esistere ostacoli" (pedestrian; DM 8.2.1). There is no vehicle or parkade headroom.
7. **Contrast, light and sound are unquantified.** The texts have "variazioni cromatiche" and "differenziato per materiale e colore", stair "illuminazione artificiale" with no illuminance, and no reverberation, LRV or colour temperature at all. Slip resistance, by contrast, is a defined term with a friction coefficient and a named test method.
8. **One word, two elements.** "corrimano" names both the stair handrail and the toilet grab rail, beside "maniglione". "rampa" is both a ramp and a stair flight ("rampa di scale"). "ripiano" (ramp/path landing) differs from "pianerottolo" (stair landing).
9. **The population is functional and constant.** "persone con ridotta o impedita capacità motoria o sensoriale" and "persona su sedia a ruote" are the reference users. DPR 503 adds "persone che si muovono lentamente". A barrier is defined to include *missing* orientation cues for "non vedenti … ipovedenti … sordi" (DM art. 2 A c).
10. **Assistance as a compliance state.** "visitabilità condizionata" (DM 5.7) and the DPR 503 "sistema di chiamata per attivare un servizio di assistenza" make staff help, signalled by a symbol, a recognised interim state.
11. **Ratio is a share of units.** "almeno il 5% degli alloggi" and accessible rooms "almeno due fino a 40" are shares of accessible units, not a mix of room types.

## Defects found in the METHOD
- **M1. Normattiva's article text can be wrong.** DM 4.1.9 on Normattiva reads "I corridoi devono presentare variazioni di livello"; the DPR 503 Appendice reads "non devono". The next clause supports "non". A second witness should be required wherever two texts exist. The GU scan, the authentic text, was not OCR'd.
- **M2. The values are in the diagrams.** The manoeuvring spaces, door approach spaces and control heights sit in graphic parts. A text-only pass reads the words around them, and "Turning circle", for example, is FOUND only from the clauses that point to a diagram.
- **M3. `retrieval_log.locate_quote` misses entity-encoded accented text.** The raw-byte check reads as 41 false "missing" results for Italian HTML. Its HTML handling needs `html.unescape` (not patched: outside this folder).
- **M4. Session-bound portals.** Normattiva's "atto completo", AKN export and caricaArticolo links all fail without a cookie. Only the per-article URN (`~artN`) works with `fetch()`. A tag-strip extractor also silently truncates at `<----` markers.
- **M5. Inherited from the pilots:** criterion-shaped register rows (D1), 2.2 quantity words as senses (D2), FOUND-PARTIAL by hand judgment (D2). The same 60-concept set as FR/DE was used for comparability. The B651 headings that RHFAC lacks (brief, Concept list) were NOT added as concepts. That is a scope gap shared with the pilots.
- **M6.** The held aliases are mostly word-for-word translations. "pendenza della rampa" (text: "pendenza delle rampe"), "valvola termostatica" (text: "miscelatori termostatici") and "soglia a filo" (text: "complanari") are OR-12b failures.
