## Extraction
- GB 55019: `pdftoppm -r 300 -gray` → `tesseract <page> -l chi_sim --psm 6` (tesseract 5.3.4, installed with apt in this container) → every page viewed at 130 dpi and the OCR corrected by hand → text/ee78b16e1dec13b8.txt. A character-level diff of the transcript against raw OCR (inline difflib, after stripping digits and punctuation) listed every change. Each change was checked against the page image; two were checked again on 300 dpi crops (箅 versus 算; the door 扶手).
- CORRECTION during the pass: my first transcription of 2.5.4(2) wrote 把手 (door handle) where the page prints 扶手 (the same word as handrail). The OCR had it right. A crop at 300 dpi showed 扶手 and the transcript was fixed. I had substituted the expected word for the printed one. That is the transcription risk this method carries, so the defect is recorded rather than hidden.
- OCR silently lost or merged lines (it read 斜面过渡，和斜面 and the outer 1.0.x text with 无障得), but no clause turned out to be missing once each page was compared with its image.
- Law: tags stripped from the 12371 HTML. All 16 law quotes in observations and reverse-harvest were found in the raw HTML bytes by `retrieval_log.locate_quote` (kind 'unscoped', because no ref_id is attached). The 12371 page is a republication (源: 新华社), not the NPC gazette.
