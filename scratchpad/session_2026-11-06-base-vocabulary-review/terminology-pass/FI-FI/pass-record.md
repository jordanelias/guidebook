## Where Finnish carves the field differently (instrument-derived; glosses ours)
1. **One route object, "kulkuväylä".** It names the approach route (2 §), the interior route (5 §), and the ramp, which OH18 1.3 defines as "kulkuväylän osa". The decree has **no corridor and no minimum interior route width**. A width under 1 500 mm only triggers turning spaces every 15 m. "käytävä" occurs in the decrees only inside "porraskäytävä" (stairwell) and "uloskäytävä" (exit route). The held seed "käytävän leveys" occurs nowhere.
2. **Ramp run is limited by rise, not length.** "yhtäjaksoisen luiskan korkeusero" may be at most 500 mm at the steeper slope. The 8 % option is gated by total rise (≤1 000 mm). Parameter 12 has no Finnish counterpart as a length.
3. **Populations are a two-part defined carve.** "liikkumis- ja toimimisesteinen henkilö" covers both groups. "Toimimisesteisiä" includes seeing, hearing, remembering, understanding and perceiving ("hahmottaminen") (OH18 1.3). The rollator user ("pyörillä varustettu kävelyteline") is named in the decree beside the wheelchair user throughout, and so is the assistant ("avustaja").
4. **Contrast is "tummuus" (darkness), never quantified.** K17 says "valoisuuserot" and "tummuuserojen". OH18 says "tummuuskontrasti" and pairs it with "tuntokontrasti" (underfoot or cane-felt). There is no LRV and no luminance term. Contrast, lighting and stairs sit in the *safety* decree, not the accessibility decree.
5. **Climate enters the geometry.** An outdoor ramp may exceed 5 % only if kept "sisätilassa olevaan luiskaan verrattavassa kunnossa" ("katettu tai sulanapidetty", OH18). Outdoor stair going and rise change with "katettu/lämmitetty". The entrance "shelter" exists only as a canopy against snow drift ("kinostuminen", K17 18 §).
6. **Stairs are "nousu" and "etenemä"**, governed first by their ratio ("suhde"). "nousu" is the rise quantity, not the riser element. The open-riser concept is a 100 mm test-cube gap limit.
7. **Clear space is a disc and a volume.** "vapaa tila" is given by "halkaisija" (diameter) and, in OH18, extends "lattiatasosta 2000 mm korkeuteen". Transfer space beside the WC carries handedness ("kätisyys").
8. **Adaptability is a legal state.** "varustettavissa" (can be equipped later) is distinct from "varustettava" (must be equipped). A 5 % share rule applies to student, youth and hotel rooms.
9. **Drafting.** The decree states prescriptions with performance clauses ("helposti havaittava", "helposti avata") and a renovation proportionality test ("tarkoituksenmukaista"). Application is decided by the "kunnan rakennusvalvontaviranomainen". It has no aim/prescription split like the French text. Small houses get only 2–4 §.

## Defects found in the METHOD
- D1. **Finnish morphology defeats substring tests both ways.** Inflection and consonant gradation hide true hits: "tukikaide" vs "tukikaiteiden", "kynnyksetön" vs "Kynnyksettömissä". Compounds also produce false hits: "käytävä" inside "porraskäytävä". The 25/160 alias figure is therefore unreliable in both directions. The FR method (lower-case `in`) does not transfer to agglutinative languages. A lemmatised or stem test is needed.
- D2. **The raw-byte check cannot see into PDFs.** `locate_quote` reports every OH18 quote as missing. Verification for that text rests on pdftotext.
- D3. **Finlex currency trap.** The open-data `fin@` expression is the 2017 text, even though its metadata says it was consolidated in 2024. The current text needs the version suffix (`fin@20240683`). The HTML pages are client-rendered shells.
- D4. **Registry and base defects (not corrected).** `standards-registry.md` has two FI entries. Neither records 683/2024 or rakentamislaki, and one calls it "F1", the retired RakMK code. REF-01017's title lacks diacritics. Every held fi alias has jurisdiction NULL.
- D5. The AKN-to-text helper (`tools/akn2t.py`) is ad hoc. `retrieval_log` records no "mirror of" field for OH18.
- D6. Held word-for-word seeds absent from all three texts include "käytävän leveys", "pyörätuolin kääntösäde", "pyörähdysympyrä", "oviaukon leveys" and "avausvoima". The decree's own word is "kääntymistila". These are the OR-12b failure.
