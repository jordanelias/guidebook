**Owner classes in English, by jurisdiction**
- *Parking.* US: Accessible Parking Spaces (ADA 208), RESERVED PARKING for persons with disabilities (MUTCD 2B.46), handicapped parking / Parking spaces for the handicapped (NY VTL § 1203-b). CA: Designated accessible parking (B651 9.4), accessible parking space (ON Reg. 581, Toronto), barrier-free parking spaces (Sault Ste. Marie). UK: parking bay designated for disabled people (ADM 1.18), disabled badge holders only (DfT signs, DfI NI), Blue Badge (GOV.UK). IE: designated car(-)parking spaces (TGD M), disabled persons parking bay (S.I. 182/1997). AU: accessible carparking spaces (Premises Standards D3.5), disability parking permit (WA), disabled parking bay (Darwin), disabled parking spaces (Glen Eira), accessibility parking (Darebin). NZ: accessible parks (D1/AS1 comment), mobility parking spaces / mobility parks (University of Auckland, CCS Disability Action). SG: accessible (vehicle) parking lots (BCA Code 3.3.1), disabled car park lots (MSF).
- *Vertical transport.* Only the US and Canada say elevator (ADA 407, B651 5.6.1, ON O. Reg. 209/01 "passenger elevator"). UK, IE, AU and SG say passenger lift. In CA, Ontario's class "Lifts for persons with physical disabilities" covers platform and stair lifts. IE uses both "platform lift" and "lifting platform".
- *Umbrella qualifier.* US: Accessible (defined, 106.5). CA: barrier-free (Accessible Canada Act long title) alongside accessible (Act name, Reg. 581). UK: disabled for people and badges, accessible and wheelchair-accessible for rooms. IE: Universal Design (Disability Act 2005 definition, cited in TGD M) plus accessible. SG: accessible, inclusive, barrier-free and Universal Design in one code. AU: accessible, plus "people with a disability". "wheelchair-friendly" is attested once (Bellingen, NSW).
