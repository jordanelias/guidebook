The critical methodology comes from **synthesis, adjudication and specifications**, not from evidence collection.

**Why:** owner said so (2026-10-29) while directing the ramp-slope batch. Collection is the input stage; what makes the guidebook credible is how conflicting codes, studies, professional-body and advocacy positions are weighed and turned into a specification.

**How to apply:** gather evidence efficiently (accept sources as read, do not over-audit collection, see [[code-baselines-accepted]] and [[current-state-only]]). Spend design and review effort on the adjudication rules (how jurisdictions, studies, professional bodies and advocacy groups are weighed), the synthesis logic, and the specification output per tier/population. Revisits the stress-test grading, which weighted collection and verification heavily; judgment and synthesis stages are where gaps matter most. See [[project-end-goal]].
