# Workplan against the ideal state graph: comparison and adjudications

**Status:** PROPOSED · **Date:** 2026-10-12 · **Compares:** `workplan/2026-10-12-simplification-workplan.md` (v3.2) and `workplan/pipeline-state-graph/IDEAL-GRAPH.md` (the Opus design, after two antagonist passes). **Adjudications** are made on the owner's instruction of 2026-10-12 ("wherever there is a conflict, adjudicate to ensure 100% accuracy, code compliance and logic for ensuring walkability in pipeline as well as dynamic information collection") and are open to the owner's override. Doctrine text (mission, tier definitions) stays an owner merge. Facts marked *(checked)* I re-ran; facts marked *(pass)* come from an antagonist's report and I did not re-run them. This file was corrected after the fifth and sixth passes, which applied its adoptions to scratch clones: five of them failed there (P1, P6, P11, P12, P18) and are withdrawn or deferred below.

## 1. Verdict

Neither document is adopted as it stands. **The ideal is the better shape and an unreachable destination as written.** Built against the live data, 28 of 53 admitted sources, 30 of 88 observations and 25 to 101 of 101 extractions would not load *(pass)*; its derivations of admissions, slug links and the follow-up tag are false on live rows (P3, P13); it drops a live guard (identifier reissue) and live columns without replacing them (P9, P10, P14). The failures are of two kinds, and the remedy differs: where the **rows** fail a ratified rule (sources without persisted bytes against R10 and CLAUDE.md 5(c); 25 sources with no `source_type`), the rows are to be repaired and the ideal's constraint held at the end; where the **design** misstates a fact the rows record, the design is wrong and is rejected. **The plan is reachable and deletes first, but it leaves the owner's walkability requirement partly unmet:** after its Phase 1, 21 of the remaining 58 tables fail the walkability script. After the whole v3 course, 17 or 18 tables still fail, the specification-to-render cell is empty, and the walk from a figure back to its retrieved bytes does not exist, because the bytes-pointer design the first version of this file adopted does not survive a rebuild (P11). The judgment-to-synthesis cell is empty only for adjacent foreign keys: the walk exists in two hops through the specification (P1).

What survives rehearsal and is adopted: the four lens columns on `search_executions` (P2), the walkability check and centralized bindings (P20, P21), and the deletions both documents agree on. Everything else the ideal adds is withdrawn, or deferred behind a named owner ruling or a design that is owed. The ideal's chief use is as a list of what is missing, not as a list of what to build.
