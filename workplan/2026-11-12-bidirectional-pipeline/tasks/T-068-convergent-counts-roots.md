### What the investigation looks at

_Design text: the specification of a read-only diagnostics reader that this task does **not** build (the task is owed: `OPEN-ITEMS-PLAN.md` OI-57). The owner asked (2026-11-14): "different values because different ICF/access need/identity targeted? or is there a clause somewhere that hasn't been picked up that modifies the parameter? are there conditions? is it step-based? triggered?", and added "other reasons for divergence vs convergence may be residential vs non-residential, fully universal design vs limited mobility design, etc", and "it may also speak towards RANGES of values / eg a mirror mounted between 1000mm aff to 1200mm"._

For each divergent parameter (the queue of item 6) and each pair of its clusters, the reader prints the typed attributes that **differ** between the two clusters, read from rows already held, and nothing it would have to curate (rule 8). Every check reads an existing column or relation; none adds one. **It is a starting list the reader prints, not a closed vocabulary** (the owner's "etc"):
- (a) **Groups targeted:** the lens links (identity, ICF, access need, medical: `v_live_lens_links`). Different groups targeted mean the clusters belong in separate cells, not one.
- (b) **Conditions:** condition edges (`extraction_relations` rows with `relation` `condition_on`) and `figure_role` `condition` rows: a conditioned figure.
- (c) **Setting:** the `setting` text, and, once T-091 lands, the typed circumstance it carries.
- (d) **Source attributes:** `device_class`, `measurement_paradigm`, `jurisdiction`, publication year (`evidence_sources.pub_year`), tier, and root (`root_ref_id`, `root_id`, `root_type`, `echo_of`).
- (e) **Step-based:** several rows of one parameter from one source that form a stepped series (a tested set of values, `extraction_relations` `tested_at`, or a table of steps) read as competing claims.
- (f) **Triggered:** a threshold or trigger comparator: a `>` or `<` figure that is the point above which a rule applies, not a value of the parameter (the first Edmonton figure, "1 in 20", was this case).
- (g) **Building use:** residential against non-residential (and public, commercial), read from the typed circumstance OD-49 keeps (building use and interior or exterior, narrowed by the thirteenth set's answer 3) once T-091 lands (`workplan/2026-11-12-bidirectional-pipeline/tasks/T-091-typed-circumstance-carrier.md`), and from `setting` until then; pointed at, never copied (rule 5).
- (h) **Design mode:** fully universal design against a design for limited mobility or another named group: `specifications.design_scale` (`universal`, `population`, `person`) on the determination's side, and the extraction's lens links (T-018, T-019) on the evidence's side. Two sources can differ because one targets everyone and the other a named group; that is a reason to read the clusters apart, not a defect in either.
- (i) **Ranges:** are the divergent clusters the bounds of one range another source states (a mirror mounted 1000 to 1200 mm above finished floor)? The reader prints every held interval (item 5, from `bound_on_scale`, no new column) that is consistent with both clusters, as spanning them.
- (j) **An uncaptured clause:** where none of (a) to (i) differs, the reader reports the cause as **undetermined**, which is itself the cue for a human read, and prints the claim texts (`claim_text`, `value_as_written`) and their context quotes (`extraction_relations.quote`) with the modifier words found in them (where, when, if, unless, except, for, up to, at least, per, each, until, provided, only), so the investigator can see whether a clause that modifies the parameter was not captured (a follow-up extraction or a new condition row follows, through `db.py`).

**The modifier-word screen is the one list**, and it is a screen, not a vocabulary: it only chooses which words to highlight in text that is printed whole, so a word missing from it hides nothing. It lives in the reader alone, is maintained by whoever owns the reader (its task file names the owner), and is read by nothing else. **The output** is what the investigation queue entry points an investigator to; its disposition is the investigator's (a new condition row, a lens correction, separate cells, a synthesis approach, or a recorded finding that the field disagrees), never the reader's.
