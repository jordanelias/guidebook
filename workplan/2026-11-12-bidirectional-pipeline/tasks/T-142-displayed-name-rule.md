## 3. The problem, in the guidebook's terms

A concept's displayed name is the name a page prints for it. Today `v_term_names` picks it by form number (`MIN(form_id)`), so a lower-numbered form recorded later, or any second English code, renames the concept with no judge; the ledger forbids exactly that ("Do not choose the displayed name by `MIN(form_id)` or by recency", ACTION (2) of "Owner statements 2026-11-18 — OD-71 …"). The owner's rule (OD-72, ratified 2026-11-19) counts the attested appearances of each way of writing the name, puts a CSA or ISO form first, shows our label and owes a judge on a tie, and lets a named judge's pick override. Two facts it needs are not held: which body issued the instrument an occurrence was read in (CSA has no row anywhere; a URL substring is not a warrant), and a grouping key no view can compute (SQLite's `lower()` folds ASCII only, and the project's one normaliser, `retrieval_log.normalise_quote`, applies NFKC, which SQL cannot).

**Evidence command** (read-only):
