Reg. 163/24) under `CA` and REF-00995 (Besluit van de Vlaamse Regering) under `BE`. **Verified.**

## OD-02 — The language of a page; how a translated quotation is shown

### A. Fact check

- **No ruling sets a page language.** `git grep -n -i -E "page language|language of (the|a) page|pages in english|written in english" -- governance references/project-standards.md decisions sessions` finds nothing on point. **Verified.**
- **But an owner statement bears on it directly and is not cited.** PS:5222, the first owner statement of 2026-11-12: "ensure that our pipeline can be run in all directions at any stage in such a manner where we can dynamically research, adjudicate, synthesize, specify and **render across a multitude of languages** and jurisdictions." The resolutions file and the ledger entry for OD-02 do not mention it.
- **Ninth statement (PS:5358):** "probably at synthesis or whatever we need to actually translate everything". Quoted correctly; the resolution's use (quotations may be translated) matches the register's narrowing (REMEDIATION-WORKPLAN, OD-02).
- **Eighth statement of 2026-11-06 (PS:5200-5215).** The owner's words are "you obviously can have English words for these rows." and "we are trying to avoid english BIAS". The resolutions file quotes the recording session's gloss (PS:5209: "English must not be the pivot that decides which concepts exist, what the standard form is, or how a non-English concept is described") and then, in the criteria test and in the ledger entry, narrows it to "which concepts exist and which column is read first". The narrowing drops "how a non-English concept is described", which is exactly what an English translation beside a quotation does. The quoted sentence is the recorder's reading, not the owner's verbatim, but the owner's own words ("avoid english BIAS") are wider still, not narrower.
- **WCAG 2.2 SC 3.1.2 Language of Parts** is Level AA, inside the owner's WCAG 2.2 AA requirement (PS:4847, 4858). **Verified.**
- `governance/audience-priority.md` names four audiences and no language (the resolver says so). The resolver's statement that the audiences "include readers in JP, KR, CN, DE, FR" is an inference from P1+P2+P3 scope, not a recorded audience.

### B. Authority check

- The resolution does not override a ruling: no ruling fixes a page language, and keeping the page language a render parameter leaves "render across a multitude of languages" reachable.
- It does, however, settle in practice a reading of the owner's own words (PS:5222) that the ledger reserved as an owner question (PS:5236). Under the delegation that is allowed only as a reading under condition (c) ("a defensible reading and not a statement of what the owner meant"). The resolution is not labelled as one, and does not name the statement it reads.
- The anti-English-bias rulings: English pages with the original shown first or beside, language-tagged, is a defensible way to avoid bias on the reading surface. The ledger sentence that claims those rulings do not reach the reading surface at all overstates; it should claim only that this design meets them.

### C. Criteria check

The C1-over-C2 call rests on the unratified priority assumption, as the resolver says. One argument against option (ii) is overstated: "every fixed sentence of every page would then be unreviewed translated prose of our own". Under the resolver's own OD-12, fixed sentences are a bounded set of templates, translatable once per language, and under its own OD-30 a translation can be reviewed by a second Opus-class instance before a reader sees it. So (ii), or a hybrid (English plus one further page language), is cheaper and less C1-hostile than portrayed. A reader who weights C2 or C3 more, or who reads PS:5222 as requiring pages in several languages, would reach a hybrid. Contestability: moderate. The stated confidence ("medium on English as the only page language") is honest.

### D. Consequence check

Releases T-037 (render) and T-112 (target language English). Nothing is published yet (pre-launch), and translations are additive rows attached by pointer, so reversal is cheap. One risk the resolution creates: a "page language parameter" with a single value and no test that exercises a second value is a hard-coded behaviour in effect (seventh statement of 2026-11-12, PS:5322: "pipeline must not have hard coded behaviours"). A second risk: T-112's English translations become the medium through which synthesis compares non-English evidence ("so that it can be compared and discussed together", PS:5360). That makes English the comparison pivot at synthesis, a stage choice the ninth statement left open. OD-02 should bind the page only.

### Verdict: CONFIRMED WITH CORRECTIONS

1. Ledger entry and resolutions file: add that this is **a reading of the owner's statement of 2026-11-12 (PS:5222, "render across a multitude of languages") under condition (c)**, namely that rendering across languages is met by showing each source's own language, tagged, beside our translation, with the page language a parameter, and not by pages in several languages in this edition. Name PS:5236 as the place the question was reserved.
2. Ledger "What decided it": replace "English pages are not the English bias ruled against on 2026-11-06, which concerns which concepts exist and which column is read first" with a sentence that does not narrow the ruling, for example: "English pages are held to the 2026-11-06 rulings against English bias by showing the original first or beside every translation, by never presenting our English as a source's term, and by keeping English one column of the matrix; they are not exempt from those rulings."
3. ACTION (3) gains a test: the renderer must emit a page in a second language by data alone (a template set and a page-language value), with no code edit, so the parameter is not dead (seventh statement).
4. Scope the English target to display: "T-112's target language is English for display; this resolution does not fix the language in which synthesis compares evidence."
5. Resolutions file C2 bullet: remove or correct "every fixed sentence of every page would then be unreviewed translated prose of our own" in the light of OD-12 (bounded templates) and OD-30 (review before display).

## OD-14 — Carrying the rows written under `MOB` since the split was ratified

### A. Fact check

- **The ruling (PS:1291-1313).** The owner's words are only: *"yes, AMB and WHEEL to fan out."* The operative scope is "All **31** `item_population_links` rows keyed `MOB` … become **62** rows". The strict §2.4 alternative — delete all 31 as pre-synthesis presupposition … One-word decision: 'fan' or 'drop'." The alternative put to the owner was *drop*, not *judge*. The design's reason for fan-out (§2.1) is anti-erasure and conservation: "assigning each row to only one member is a per-item judgment nobody has made; making it silently would erase one community from each item". It also notes that the 31 rows had `rationale_ref=NULL` and came from "the retired `applicable_groups` CSV": scaffold, not evidence.
- **The overruling (PS:2000-2003),** owner 2026-08-27: "overrule Aug 24 DR. we go evidence>judgment>synthesis." Its ACTION (1) supersedes "*applicability is an OUTPUT of synthesis*" and "*zero `item_population_links` is the correct pre-synthesis state*", and places the crossing at judgment. It does not mention the mobility split or its fan-out. **Verified as quoted;** the resolver's reading that it removed §2.4 is right; its reading that it thereby removed *the owner's* reason for fan-out is not, because the owner gave none, and the reason the design gave (anti-erasure) is untouched.

- **Misattributed quotation.** The resolutions file (C1 bullet) says a fan-out row is, "by the ruling's own words, 'provisionally wrong for one of the two groups'". That phrase is the register's (`git grep -n -F "provisionally wrong" -- references governance workplan decisions` finds it only in REMEDIATION-WORKPLAN.md, OD-14 option (a)). The ruling's words are "not a judgement that every demand applies equally to both".
- **When the rows were written.** `python3 -I -c "import sqlite3;c=sqlite3.connect('file:data/guidebook.db?mode=ro',uri=True);print(c.execute(\"select min(created_at),max(created_at),count(*) from source_value_extractions where identity_code='MOB'\").fetchall())"`: every `MOB` figure postdates both the split and the 2026-08-27 overruling. - **Registry state.** `select identity_code,status from base_taxonomy_identity where identity_code in ('MOB','AMB','WHEEL')` returns only `MOB`, `active`; the status vocabulary is `active`/`deprecated` (its CHECK), so "retired in place" means `deprecated`. `evidence_population_match` has no retirement column (`PRAGMA table_info(evidence_population_match)`), so "the old grade rows are retired in place" needs a schema change the resolution does not name.
- **Not cited, and supporting judging for provisions:** owner 2026-11-06 (second), item 2 at PS:4935 and ACTION (2) at PS:4941: "Do not tag a provision from an accessibility code ALL because its sentence names no group; leave the tag to the lens fill", and the recorded reading "Which group a provision carries is judgment's output, filled with the link table".

### B. Authority check

1. **The decisive argument is an implied retirement of an owner ruling.** The ledger's "What decided it" rests on: the ruling chose fan-out because judging would infer applicability before synthesis; §2.4 was overruled; so the ruling's reason is gone. Three defects. (i) The reason is the recorder's gloss, and it misstates the choice the owner made (fan or *drop*). (ii) The reason the design actually gave, anti-erasure, survives the overruling intact. (iii) Even if the reason had lapsed, a delegate concluding that an owner rule no longer binds because a later ruling removed its basis, where the later ruling does not name it, is the act the delegation reserved. OD-13 is reserved for exactly this ("Only the owner retires an owner rule where the later ruling does not name it", REMEDIATION-WORKPLAN OD-13), and condition (e) forbids a delegated resolution to override a standing ruling. **As written, this is a case that must be referred back.**
2. **A sound ground exists and is under-used.** The ruling's operative words are scoped to 31 `item_population_links` rows (a table since dropped); its only forward-looking clause concerns grades, and OD-14 keeps it; the rows now at issue are post-ruling extraction rows that the ruling never addressed.
