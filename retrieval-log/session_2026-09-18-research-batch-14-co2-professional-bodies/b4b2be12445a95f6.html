Resources

Welcome to the WFOT Resources: A comprehensive and continuously updated source of information about occupational therapy practice and the WFOT, aimed at the occupational therapy community. Here you will find the WFOT Position Statements; the WFOT Minimum Standards for the Education of Occupational Therapists; manuals and guidelines; policies; promotional materials; online modules and a wealth of other useful publications.

Please note that to view and access certain documents, you will need to be logged in as an Individual Member.
