# DR-2026-08-01 — Research contract: single source, and three divergences resolved

**Category:** D-METH (research methodology) with a D-OP component (enforcement mechanics)
**Delegation:** DG-REVIEW — proposed and executed by the session, owner review invited
**Status:** ACCEPTED
**Supersedes:** nothing. **Extends:** DR-2026-07-25-research-contract-mechanical-enforcement
**Doctrine SHA at authoring:** `0f2f525`

---

## 1. Context

DR-2026-07-25 adopted the R1–R15 research contract on the owner's directive that
*"RESEARCH IS INVALID IF IT IS NOT COMPLIANT WITH OUR GOVERNANCE AND VERIFICATION TOOLS AND
RULES AND ETHOS"*, and made enforcement mechanical rather than remembered.

The 2026-08-01 contract inventory established that R1–R15 existed in **exactly two
hand-transcribed copies with no comparator**:

| | Location | Role |
|---|---|---|
| A | `.claude/settings.json` SessionStart hook | injected into every session |
| B | `scripts/audit/research_batch_dod.py` docstring | the enforcer's own rule table |

Neither referenced the other. They had drifted, and **two of the three divergences changed what
the contract obliges** — not how it was worded.

This is the same defect that `scripts/ci_helpers/commit_gate.py` was extracted to end for the
commit gates, and that `governance/check-registry.yaml` exists to end for check lists. It had
simply not been noticed at the contract that governs whether research counts at all.

## 2. Decision

**2.1** `governance/research-contract.yaml` becomes the **single canonical source** of R1–R15.

**2.2** The SessionStart hook payload becomes a **derived artifact**, generated by
`scripts/generate/research_contract_hook.py` and gated by `research_contract_sync`
(advisory during shakedown, per the house norm). This is the same shape as the repo's existing
derived-output gates.

**2.3** The enforcer is cross-referenced rather than generated — its rules are *implemented*,
not transcribed, so what is checkable is that the two vocabularies agree: a rule the contract
defines must be one the enforcer knows, and vice versa. Verified by mutation.

**2.4** The three divergences are resolved as below. In each case the **operative source
governs**, per CLAUDE.md §5: `references/project-standards.md` is the append-only operative rule
ledger and is preferred over older restatements.

## 3. The three resolutions

### R1 — pass composition: **Co-1 / T2 / Co-2**, not Co-1 / Co-2

Both copies read "Co-1/Co-2 LIVED EXPERIENCE pass FIRST". The cited source,
`skills/multilingual-research_SKILL.md` Step 1, reads:

> **Step 1 — Co-1 / Tier 2 / Co-2 pass (first; no exceptions)** … Co-1 (DPO/lived experience),
> Tier 2 (disability-led NGO/advocacy), and Co-2 (OT professional body CPGs).

Tier 2 here is **disability-led NGO/advocacy publication**, retrieved directly from publication
pages in native-language terms. Both copies had narrowed a three-part obligation to two parts.

**Resolved:** restored to the source. Note this does not change Co-1's co-primacy with T1 under
CRPD Art. 4.3 — it restores a third leg that was dropped, and does not touch the tier ladder.

### R2 — mining scope: **confirmed Tier 1–2**, not T1–T3

The hook obliged mining "admitted T1-T3 anchors (depth 2-3)". The operative ledger says:

> RULE: Forward + backward citation mining mandatory for every confirmed **Tier 1-2** source.

**Resolved:** T1–T2 governs. The hook had been imposing work on a tier band the rule ledger does
not require — a real cost levied on every session by a transcription error.

On depth: "depth 2-3" and `gap-driven-mining_SKILL.md`'s "**Depth-1 enforced (both modes)**"
read as a contradiction and are not one. Depth 2-3 is the traversal target for `citation-miner`.
Depth-1 is a **per-pass** bound in gap-driven mode, where discovered sources are inserted but
their networks are explicitly handed off rather than mined inline. Both are now stated in the
contract so the apparent conflict cannot be rediscovered as a defect.

### R3 — locator class: **both**, split as R3a/R3b

The hook required *clause/section/page*; the enforcer required *DOI + page/table (or direct URL)*.
Neither was wrong. They describe **different source classes**, and each copy had kept only one:

| Claim class | Locator | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Quantified **outcome** claims (percentages, n= counts, reduction figures) in BPC files | DOI + page/table, or a direct URL | `project-standards.md` RULE 2026-04-09 |
| Quantified **code / standard** values | clause / section / page | CLAUDE.md §6; standards are not DOI-bearing |

**Resolved:** both stated, both flagging `[UNVERIFIED-QUANT]` when absent. The ledger's
two-failed-attempts termination rule applies to the outcome-claim branch.

## 4. What this does not decide

- **No rule's substance is changed** beyond restoring R1's third leg and narrowing R2's tier
  band to the ledger's. R3 gains nothing new; it stops losing half of what already existed.
- The `research-contract-baseline.json` debt ratchet is untouched.
- The `Stop` hook remains non-blocking. Making the definition-of-done gate blocking is a
  separate decision and is deliberately not taken here.

## 5. Falsification

This decision is wrong if any of the following holds, and each is checkable:

- `skills/multilingual-research_SKILL.md` Step 1 is not the source R1 derives from — in which
  case R1's restored third leg is an invention.
- A later RULE in `project-standards.md` supersedes the Tier 1-2 mining scope with a wider one.
  The ledger is append-only, so this is a search, not a judgement.
- The two R3 branches are in fact one rule applied to one class, making the split ceremony.
